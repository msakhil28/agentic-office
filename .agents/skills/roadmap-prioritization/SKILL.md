Compare options against the stated objective and horizon. Record the affected users, evidence of the problem, estimated effort range, dependencies, and confidence for each option.

Make tradeoffs explicit. Separate items ready to build from items that first need validation. Identify the smallest experiment that would change the ranking and record when to revisit the decision.
