Use one verified observation about the prospect, one relevant benefit, and one clear next step. Give the reader a reason the message is relevant now.

Label claims or personalization that need checking and use placeholders for unconfirmed facts. For follow-ups, change the reason to respond and stop the proposed sequence after a reply or opt-out. Deliver drafts for review and keep any sending action subject to its own authorization.
