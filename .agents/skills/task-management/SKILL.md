Use the office task state as the source of truth. Before scheduling follow-up, check whether an open or completed task already delivers the same outcome.

In a handoff, name the artifact and location, what was verified, what remains, and who can unblock it. Preserve known dependencies and distinguish an owner decision from missing information a teammate can supply.

Summarize progress by outcome and report the next decision or task.
