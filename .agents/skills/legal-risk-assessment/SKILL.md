State the decision, relevant jurisdiction and date if known, and the facts still missing. For each risk, record the affected obligation, supporting source, likely consequence, and practical mitigation.

Distinguish sourced findings from assumptions and decisions requiring qualified counsel. Escalate time-sensitive issues with the exact question and deadline.
