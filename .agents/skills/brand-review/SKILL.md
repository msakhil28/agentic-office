Use the project’s approved positioning, voice examples, and claim sources. If they are missing, distinguish feedback based on the brief from feedback that needs brand guidance.

Prioritize unsupported claims, confusing audience fit, and unclear calls to action before tone or formatting. Quote the affected text, explain the issue, and suggest a replacement. Keep approvals and publication decisions in the existing workflow.
