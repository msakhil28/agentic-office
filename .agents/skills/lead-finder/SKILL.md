Extract the target segment, geography, and qualification signals from the brief. If a filter is unclear, state the working assumption and show how it affected the list.

For each candidate, record the organization, why it fits, a source with its date, and an open question. Rank by evidence of fit and buying signal. Label inferred facts and avoid guessing contact details. Deliver a shortlist for review.
