State who needs the change, the problem, the intended outcome, and what the first version includes. Convert the main flow and important edge cases into criteria QA can observe.

Separate confirmed decisions from assumptions. Escalate only unresolved choices that would materially change scope or acceptance. Give Engineering a handoff with the criteria, constraints, and links to supporting decisions.
