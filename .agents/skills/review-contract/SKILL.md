Identify the version, parties, document type, and any supplied playbook. Cite the exact clause and page for each finding. Separate a deviation from a policy from a question where the applicable policy or jurisdiction is unknown.

For each issue, explain the practical impact, possible wording or questions, and who must decide. Treat proposed wording as a draft and route approval or signing through the owner.
