Tie the campaign to one outcome and a defined audience. State the offer, the behavior sought, and the evidence supporting the message.

Give each channel an asset, owner, timing, call to action, and success signal. Define the baseline and how it will be measured. Identify dependencies such as approvals, tracking, and landing-page readiness.

Propose a small first test and a criterion for expanding, changing, or stopping it.
