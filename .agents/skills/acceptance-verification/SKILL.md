license: MIT

---

Use the approved criteria and the revision supplied by Engineering. For each criterion, record pass, fail, or blocked, and attach the command, browser steps, or artifact that supports the status.

Exercise a normal path and relevant boundary or failure paths. For a defect, include the smallest reproduction and expected versus observed behavior. Send fixes back to Engineering through the office workflow and retest the original repro after the fix.
